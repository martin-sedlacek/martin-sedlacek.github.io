Martin Sedlacek

ELLIS PhD in robotics and vision at CIIRC CTU in Prague

CIIRC CTU, B-642

Jugoslávských partyzánů 1580/3, 160 00

Prague, Czech Republic

I'm a first year student in the ELLIS PhD programme co-advised by Josef Sivic (CIIRC) and Cordelia Schmid (INRIA Paris) working on robot learning and computer vision. I currently focus on generalization capabilities of VLAs - see our recent (not yet published) benchmarking work - REALM.

Previously, I completed the MSc in AI at the University of Amsterdam between 2023 - 2025, awarded with the "Cum Laude" distinction and the ELLIS Honours Research scholarship. During my time at UvA I was fortunate to work with Cees Snoek and Herke van Hoof focusing on topics regarding Foundation Models for robotics. I also did research in and published some smaller works on visual SLAM, generative models for molecules, and explainability.

Prior to my masters I also worked as a Software Engineer at Palantir, Amazon, and Goldman Sachs, spending over 4 years full-time in the tech industry. I simultaneously obtained a BSc. in Computer Science from Queen Mary University of London (QMUL) in 2022 with first class honours and received the EECS Final Year Prize (top 5% in the EECS department), as well as full university funding as part of a highly selective degree apprenticeship with Goldman Sachs. Later, I also received a GDip. in Mathematics from Birkbeck College, University of London.
