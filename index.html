ELLIS PhD in robotics and vision at CIIRC, CTU in Prague

CIIRC CTU, B-642

Jugoslávských partyzánů 1580/3, 160 00

Prague, Czech Republic

I'm a first year student in the ELLIS PhD programme co-advised by Josef Sivic (CIIRC) and Cordelia Schmid (INRIA Paris) working on robot learning and computer vision. I currently focus on generalization capabilities of VLAs - see our recent benchmarking work - REALM.

Previously, I completed the MSc in AI at the University of Amsterdam awarded with the "Cum Laude" distinction and received the ELLIS Honours Research scholarship.

Between 2018 and 2023, I also worked as a Software Engineer (Palantir, Amazon, Goldman Sachs) and simultaneously completed my undergraduate studies in Computer Science and Mathematics. I graduated with the EECS Final Year Prize (top 5% in the department) from Queen Mary University of London (QMUL) and received full funding for my studies as part of a degree apprenticeship with Goldman Sachs.
